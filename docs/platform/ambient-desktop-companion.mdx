Charm Desktop is your **invocation assistant**, bringing AI agents directly into your current workflow without the need to switch contexts or open a browser window.

## Instant Access, Anywhere

With Charm Desktop running silently in your system tray, your agents are always just a keystroke away. 

Press <kbd>Alt</kbd> + <kbd>Shift</kbd> + <kbd>Space</kbd> from anywhere in your operating system to instantly summon the mini-chat interface. This ambient design ensures you can maintain focus on your primary task, querying an agent or delegating work without breaking your flow.

## Fluid Context Switching

Different tasks require different experts. The desktop companion allows you to dynamically switch between different agents on the fly.

## Core Capabilities

- **Always-on Availability**: Lives in your system tray, ready to assist at a moment's notice.
- **Global Shortcut**: Use <kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>Space</kbd> to toggle the window.
- **Deep Handoff**: Seamlessly add your agents discovered on the Charm Store to the desktop environment.
- **Native Notifications**: Agents can run long-running tasks in the background and alert you via OS-native notifications when they require input or have completed their work.
- **Local Context Advantage**: Desktop agents can be granted secure, localized context—such as reading local files or interacting with your hardware—when your use case demands it.
